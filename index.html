Help

How to use it

  1. Pick a saved student, or choose their time zone under Student's time zone.
  2. Enter the date and time shown on their screenshot.
  3. The big time is when that trade happened in your time zone, along with the market session it fell in.
Saving students: click Manage students, type a name, pick their time zone, and click Add. Picking a student only changes the student's time zone, never yours.

Defaults: use Set as default and Set as my default to choose which time zones the app opens with.

Quick buttons: "Their time right now" fills in the current time for the student, and ±1 hr nudges the time. Swap zones flips the conversion direction.
Install on your device

Put it on your home screen or desktop

Installing gives you an app icon that opens full screen and works offline. It's free and takes a few seconds.
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The sage clock icon appears on your home screen.
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen, then Install).
  4. The icon appears on your home screen and in your app drawer.
  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a small monitor with a down arrow).
    No icon? In Chrome use ⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app. In Edge use ⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app.
  3. Click Install. The app opens in its own window and is added to your Start menu.
  4. Desktop shortcut: Chrome usually adds one automatically. Edge shows a Create desktop shortcut checkbox right after install. If you don't see one, in Chrome type chrome://apps in the address bar, right-click Trade Time Converter, choose Create shortcut, check Desktop, and click Create.
  5. Taskbar: while the app is open, right-click its taskbar icon and choose Pin to taskbar.
  1. Chrome: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or ⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app.
  2. Safari (macOS Sonoma or newer): choose File → Add to Dock.
  3. The app is added to your Applications folder and Launchpad. To keep it in the Dock, right-click its Dock icon and choose Options → Keep in Dock.
After installing, open the app from its icon. Your saved students and default time zones stay on your own device. To copy your student list to another device, use Manage students → Copy student list, then Import student list on the other device.
