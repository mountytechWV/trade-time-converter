# Trade Time Converter

Convert the time on a student's trade screenshot into your own time zone. It handles daylight saving time automatically, shows which market session the trade fell in (pre-market, regular, after-hours), and lets you save students with their time zones so you can pick a student and go.

## 👉 Open the app

**https://mountytechwv.github.io/trade-time-converter/**

You can use it right in your browser, or install it so it gets its own icon on your phone or desktop, opens full screen, and works offline. Installing is free and takes a few seconds. In the app, tap the **?** button for help and these same install steps.

---

## Install on iPhone or iPad

1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**. The sage clock icon appears on your home screen.

## Install on Android

1. Open the link above in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**).
4. The icon appears on your home screen and in your app drawer.

## Install on Windows

1. Open the link above in **Chrome** or **Edge**.
2. Click the **install icon** at the right end of the address bar (a small monitor with a down arrow).
   - No icon? In Chrome: **⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app**.
   - In Edge: **⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app**.
3. Click **Install**. The app opens in its own window and is added to your Start menu.
4. **Desktop shortcut:** Chrome usually adds one automatically. Edge shows a **Create desktop shortcut** checkbox right after install. If you don't see one, in Chrome type `chrome://apps` in the address bar, right-click **Trade Time Converter**, choose **Create shortcut**, check **Desktop**, and click **Create**.
5. **Taskbar:** while the app is open, right-click its taskbar icon and choose **Pin to taskbar**.

## Install on Mac

- **Chrome:** click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or **⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app**.
- **Safari (macOS Sonoma or newer):** choose **File → Add to Dock**.
- The app is added to Applications and Launchpad. To keep it in the Dock, right-click its Dock icon and choose **Options → Keep in Dock**.

## Using it

1. Pick a saved student (or choose their time zone).
2. Enter the date and time shown on their screenshot.
3. The big time is when that trade happened in **your** time zone.

**Saving students:** click **Manage students**, type a name, pick their time zone, and click **Add**.

**Defaults:** use **Set as default** / **Set as my default** to choose which time zones the app opens with.

## Your data

Your saved students and default time zones are stored only on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere. To copy your student list to another device, use **Manage students → Copy student list**, then **Import student list** on the other device.

## Updates

When a new version is posted here, installed copies update the next time you open the app with an internet connection. If it doesn't update right away, close the app and open it again.
